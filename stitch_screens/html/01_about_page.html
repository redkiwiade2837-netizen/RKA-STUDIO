About

STITCH ARCHITECTS is a research-driven architecture and urbanism practice based in Zurich. We believe that architecture is not merely about erecting standalone structures, but about carefully intervening in existing, complex environments. Our work is defined by a rigorous analytical approach, seeking to understand the historical, social, and material layers of a site before proposing a new architectural response.

The studio's philosophy centers on the concept of "stitching"—the delicate process of integrating new typologies into the established urban fabric. This involves a deliberate embrace of constraint, where site limitations become the primary generator of form. We eschew superfluous decoration in favor of tectonic clarity, employing structural logic and material honesty to achieve spatial resonance.

Our projects range from highly constrained urban infill interventions to larger infrastructural proposals. Across all scales, we maintain a commitment to durability, adaptability, and the precise articulation of detail, aiming to produce spaces that endure both physically and culturally.
